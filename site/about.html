About

Breaking the triangle since 2013.

Established in early 2013, Fire Triangle has grown into one of the largest specialized companies covering every aspect of the fire fighting & fire alarm field in Egypt — designing, supplying, installing and maintaining UL-listed, FM-approved systems to NFPA standards and the Egyptian Codes.

Our story

From Alexandria to every major project

  1. 2013

    Founded in Alexandria, serving the fire fighting & fire alarm field across Egypt.

  2. 2016

    Became an authorized distributor for several reputed international fire-protection manufacturers.

  3. 2021

    First Egypt Energy — Firex stand; Rapidrop & Waterfall agencies.

  4. 2024

    Mobiak & Velocity lines added; 487+ projects delivered.

  5. 2026

    48-strong engineering team across Alexandria & Cairo.
